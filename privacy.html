If you do not want us to place cookies on your hard drive, please consult your internet browser’s documentation for information on how to turn off and delete persistent cookies. However, if you decide not to accept cookies from us, the website may not function properly.

You can opt-out of Google Analytics for Display Advertising and customize Google Display Network ads using the Ads Settings or disable tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On.

We may collect Personal Information that you submit to us voluntarily through our website. We collect this information when you register, create and edit a profile, answer survey questions, enter contests, use certain features on the site or other methods of submitting information.   • Other Personal Information you choose to provide such as your preferences, etc.
